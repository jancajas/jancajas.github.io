Branola Voice Notetaker

Privacy policy.

Effective 17 November 2026 · Branola Voice Notetaker by BigHippo Labs

Branola is designed to record, transcribe and organize conversations on your device. You do not need a Branola account.

Recordings, transcripts and AI notes.

Your audio, transcripts, speaker labels, markers, summaries, diagrams and search data are stored in the app on your device. Speech recognition runs locally after required model files are installed. AI Summary and visual generation use Apple's on-device Foundation Models when available. Branola does not send meeting content to a BigHippo Labs transcription or AI server.

Downloads and network connections.

First-time speech-model downloads and updates require the internet. The model hosting and download providers may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address when delivering files. Those requests download model assets; they do not upload your meeting audio or transcript. Apple manages the availability and download of its system intelligence and speech assets. Purchases, restoration and sharing destinations may also use the internet.

Purchases.

Apple processes the free trial and optional lifetime purchase through the App Store. Branola reads verified StoreKit transaction information to determine access and the original trial start date. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple's privacy policy governs its payment and Apple Account services.

Microphone and app security.

Microphone permission is used to record audio when you start a recording. Optional App Lock uses the device's system authentication. Branola receives an authentication outcome, not your Face ID data or passcode.

Sharing is your choice.

Meeting content is provided to another app or service when you explicitly choose to export, email, message or share it. That destination's privacy policy applies to the copy it receives. You control which notes, diagrams or transcript content to include. Copies you export are managed outside Branola.

Analytics and tracking.

Branola does not include advertising tracking or an app-owned analytics service. We do not sell meeting data. Optional diagnostic sharing you enable through Apple system settings is handled by Apple.

Support requests.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use that information to respond and troubleshoot. Do not include confidential meeting content unless you intentionally choose to share it for support.

Deletion and device backups.

You can delete recordings and related content in the app. Recently Deleted can retain recoverable items until they are removed. App-local data is removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to device and backup behavior. Your Apple backup settings and copies you exported are managed separately; deleting content in Branola does not delete external copies.

Changes and contact.

We will update this page when the app's data handling changes. Questions about this policy can be sent to support@bighippolabs.com.
