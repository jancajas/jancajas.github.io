Branola support.

For help, email support@bighippolabs.com. Include your app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version and a short description of what happened. Please do not send recordings or meeting content unless you choose to share them for a specific support request.

Getting started.

How do I start a recording?

Complete the one-time speech-model download, choose the meeting language and tap New recording. Choose the free 7-day trial or lifetime access when asked. Obtain permission from participants before recording.

Why does model preparation take time?

Branola downloads and prepares Whisper for offline transcription on this device. Keep the app active and allow adequate storage during first-time setup. Check the stage and elapsed time. Avoid deleting the app to troubleshoot: that may remove app-local recordings.

Where are summaries and diagrams?

After Stop, the audio is saved and playable while final transcription finishes. Open Summary and choose Create Summary, or enable Automatic Summary in Settings. Add a visual when the meeting contains a supported process, timeline or decision path. Not every meeting needs a diagram.

Why is AI Summary unavailable?

Creating new summaries and visuals requires a compatible Apple Intelligence device, the feature enabled in system settings and an available model for the selected meeting language. Transcription supports a broader set of languages. Recording, transcripts, saved notes and sharing remain available independently.

How do I share notes?

Tap Share in Summary. Choose Summary, Full Notes or Next steps, include diagrams if wanted, and send through Email, Message / WhatsApp or the system share sheet. You can save a PDF, text, Markdown or HTML file. Preview is optional.

What happens after the trial?

The trial lasts seven days from its original verified purchase. It never charges automatically. A one-time lifetime purchase enables new recordings after the trial. Existing recordings, transcripts, notes, playback and exports remain accessible. An ongoing recording is not cut off at trial expiry.

How do I restore or request a refund?

Open Settings → Trial and lifetime access → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restoring or reinstalling does not restart the trial. Apple handles billing and refunds; visit reportaproblem.apple.com for purchase assistance.

How do I delete my data?

Delete recordings in Branola and review Recently Deleted for recoverable items. Device backups and copies you exported are managed separately in their respective apps or Apple settings.
